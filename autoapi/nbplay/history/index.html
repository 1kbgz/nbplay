nbplay.history¶

Undo and redo for widget state.

History watches the synced traits of a set of widgets and records every change as an undo step. Changes that land within a short window of each other (a fader drag, a method that writes several traits) share one step, transaction() groups anything explicitly, and a notebook cell boundary always ends a step. Transient traits such as play state, playhead position, meters, and request nonces are ignored.
